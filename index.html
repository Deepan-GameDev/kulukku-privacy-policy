Kulukku - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

Deycron Studios ("we", "our", or "us") operates Kulukku, a multiplayer party game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use our game.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the game and its enabled services, the following information may be processed:

  • Player Information: Player names and in-game identifiers used during multiplayer sessions.
  • Gameplay Information: Movie titles submitted by players, room codes, game sessions, scores, and gameplay activity.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, IP address, device identifiers, and technical information required by our service providers.
  • Advertising Information: Advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and related information processed by advertising SDKs and their partners.
  • Diagnostic Information: Crash reports and technical information used to improve game performance.

2. Multiplayer Services

Kulukku uses Photon Fusion 2 by Photon Engine to provide online multiplayer functionality.

Information such as player identifiers, player names, room information, and gameplay data may be transmitted through Photon services to create and manage multiplayer sessions. Information shared within a room may be visible to other players in that room.

Photon Privacy Policy: https://www.photonengine.com/en/privacy

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Kulukku uses Unity LevelPlay to manage advertisements and advertising mediation. Depending on availability and configuration, advertising may be provided through third-party networks including Google AdMob, Unity Ads, and ironSource.

These services may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions, and technical information to provide, measure, and improve advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements may provide in-game rewards such as coins or access to certain gameplay features. Watching an advertisement is optional where a rewarded advertisement is offered.

Third-party privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Photon Privacy Policy

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain multiplayer gameplay.
  • Manage rooms, player sessions, and game progress.
  • Provide advertisements and rewarded ad features.
  • Maintain game security and prevent misuse.
  • Identify technical issues and improve performance.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell players' personal information. Information may be processed or shared with service providers such as Photon Engine, Unity, Google AdMob, and advertising mediation partners when necessary to operate the game, provide advertisements, or maintain services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect the security of our services.

6. Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements and the retention practices of our service providers.

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate legal authorization. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information. You may contact us using the details below to make a privacy request.

9. Third-Party Services

Third-party services used by the game operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage users to review those policies to understand how their information is handled.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
